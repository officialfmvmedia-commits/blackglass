How to read this

Tracked wallet

An address named by a public source: the OFAC sanctions list, an FBI statement, an analyst post-mortem, or an open exchange-label dataset. Every label links to where it came from.

Exchange wallet

Hot wallets pay out withdrawals, cold wallets hold reserves, and deposit wallets collect user funds. Exchanges move huge sums every day, so a big move alone is usually routine.

What looks unusual

A hot wallet suddenly losing most of its balance, many big transfers to never-labeled addresses in an hour, or money touching a mixer or hacker wallet. That's how the Binance Oct 29 drain looked.

Score 0–100

Source ledger

METHOD. Ingestion pulls the OFAC SDN list (DPRK programs) and addresses published in FBI statements, then reads each address's recent transactions from Blockstream (BTC) and Blockscout (ETH). Clustering uses common-input co-spend on BTC, shared designation entries, and direct transfers between tracked ETH addresses. Child addresses score 0-100 on hop distance, peel-chain shape, fan-out speed, mixer, bridge or swap hops, and value. The pipeline runs about every 15 minutes on GitHub Actions.
