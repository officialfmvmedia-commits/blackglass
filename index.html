How to read this

Tracked wallet

A crypto address that an official public source (the OFAC sanctions list or an FBI statement) named as linked to North Korean hackers. We only label those.

Alert

A real transaction we saw on the blockchain involving a tracked wallet. Every alert links to the transaction and to the source.

Score 0–100

How much a movement looks like known Lazarus laundering habits. It's a pattern match, not proof of who controls a wallet.

Child / new address

A wallet that received money straight from a tracked wallet (one "hop" away). It isn't labeled as Lazarus, just flagged as worth watching.

Source ledger

METHOD. Ingestion pulls the OFAC SDN list (DPRK programs) and addresses published in FBI statements, then reads each address's recent transactions from Blockstream (BTC) and Blockscout (ETH). Clustering uses common-input co-spend on BTC, shared designation entries, and direct transfers between tracked ETH addresses. Child addresses score 0-100 on hop distance, peel-chain shape, fan-out speed, mixer, bridge or swap hops, and value. The pipeline runs about every 15 minutes on GitHub Actions.
